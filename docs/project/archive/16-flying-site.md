# 16. Flying site (flying.jerryqiu.com)

Merged 2026-10-15. Tools first, text timeline second, almost nothing personal. Phase tags as elsewhere. Builds on the Phase 2.5 flying world in [12](12-vision-v2.md) (journal, delayed airport-level map, hidden CFI slot); where they differ the owner decides (Q60).

## 1. Direction
- Recommended: a few flight tools plus a text timeline. A full ForeFlight clone is out of scope (navigation use carries real safety and liability problems). Tools are for learning only.

## 2. Pages
- P1 Home, Tools, Timeline, About (two or three lines; no school, location or photo unless chosen).
- P2 Notes (study notes you write), optional contact form that hides the email address.
- P3 Gallery, only if photos exist.

## 3. Timeline
- P1 Milestones with month and year and a one-line note (first lesson, first solo, written exam, checkride, goals); no locations or names; an icon in place of a photo.
- P2 "What I learned" notes; optional photo per milestone; optional totals-only hours counter.
- P3 Goals section with no dates required.

## 4. Tools
- P2 Tier 1: airport weather (METAR, TAF), weight and balance with sample aircraft, crosswind, density altitude.
- P3 Tier 2: trip planner (distance, heading, time, fuel), briefing board (pilot reports, G-AIRMETs, SIGMETs near a route).
- OPT Tier 3: map with airports and weather (libraries not researched).
- Not planned: chart replacement, flight plan filing, traffic, real navigation.
- Data: Aviation Weather Center Data API, called server-side and cached. Limits as pasted: 100 requests/min, 1 request/min per endpoint, 15 days of data, 400 entries (NOT VERIFIED, re-check). Credit the source. Every tool carries a learning-only notice and "get an official briefing before flying".

## 5. Notes (P3)
- Your own study notes, flashcards from your own notes (not commercial question banks), printable checklists.

## 6. Privacy
- First name or handle; no school, flight school, instructors, home airport, certificate or tail numbers; month and year only; never live location or future plans; post flights only after landing, by a day or more (the Phase 2.5 default is stricter: month precision, 7+ days, Q43). Keep opinions about schools private.

## 7. Cost and order
- Under $1/month of its own; no database; photos in Blob only if added.
- Order: Home, About, Timeline; airport weather; calculators; trip planner; briefing board and notes.

Open: name or handle; how much timeline detail is public; which tools you would use; stop at weather and calculators or build the trip planner; say "student pilot" or not.
